Mobile puzzle · 100-level curve

Pixel Unpuzzle

Gameplay: tap jigsaw pieces to send them to the matching color tray. Spare pieces wait in a 5-slot holding row, and the level fails when it fills with no valid move.

  • Game rules live in a pure C# model that emits events; Unity views only animate them.
  • DFS solver plus a generator that outputs only solvable levels.
  • Difficulty score from 0 to 100, used to order and plan a 100-level curve.
  • Custom Unity Editor level editor: paint pieces, undo/redo, one-click solve and instant play-test.

Mobile puzzle · hearts system

Arrow Puzzle

Gameplay: tap an arrow to slide it off the board in its direction. A blocked tap costs a heart, and the level is lost at zero hearts.

  • Generator that makes every level solvable by construction.
  • Solver that rates difficulty by "waves" of arrows that are free to move, and powers the hint system.
  • Ads and saving sit behind swappable interfaces; added haptics, procedural SFX and safe-area UI.
